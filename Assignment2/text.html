* Fake Inception Script * FADE IN: Waves crash. COBB lies on a shoreline, exhausted, pulled from the water. He’s dragged into a fortress and brought before an aged SAITO. SAITO (OLD) “Are you here to kill me?” COBB studies him, confused, as if remembering something half-forgotten. COBB is suddenly younger, sitting across from SAITO in a sleek room. SAITO “Do you know what it is to be a lover of architecture?” COBB explains extraction—the process of entering dreams to steal secrets. He demonstrates how the mind reveals hidden truths under subconscious pressure. SAITO “And if you can steal an idea… why not plant one instead?” COBB hesitates. COBB “Inception. It’s not possible.” But Saito insists. One idea, planted deep enough, can grow naturally. COBB returns to his reality—isolated, restless. He spins a small metal top on a table. It spins perfectly. He stares at it, haunted. Fragments of MAL interrupt his thoughts—her voice, her face, her accusation. He accepts Saito’s offer: perform inception on ROBERT FISCHER, and Saito will erase his criminal record so he can return home to his children. In Paris, Cobb meets ARIADNE, a brilliant architecture student. He tests her ability to build mazes. Later, inside a dream, she realizes the environment is unstable. The street begins to bend. Buildings fold overhead. Gravity loses meaning. ARIADNE “This isn’t real.” COBB “No… but it feels real.” He explains how dreams work: the dreamer populates the world, the subconscious defends itself, and time expands. Ariadne begins designing dream spaces but soon notices something dangerous—Mal intrudes violently into the dream. The team assembles in a warehouse: ARTHUR, precise and controlled; EAMES, a shapeshifter who can forge identities; YUSUF, who provides a powerful sedative to allow deeper dream levels; and SAITO, who oversees the mission. Cobb outlines the plan: three layers deep, each level pushing Fischer toward an emotional realization—not to follow his father, but to become his own man. On the airplane, they begin. The sedation is strong—too strong. If they die in the dream, they won’t wake up. They’ll fall into limbo. Rain hits windows. They’re in a city. Fischer is abducted into a van, but projections attack—trained, aggressive. ARTHUR “He’s been trained.” The mission becomes unstable, but Cobb insists they continue. Yusuf drives as the van speeds through traffic under heavy gunfire. They enter the next level: a hotel. Arthur manages the environment as gravity subtly shifts. Eames impersonates Fischer’s trusted associate, manipulating his emotions and guiding him toward the idea that his father’s empire must be dismantled. They descend further into a snow-covered fortress. The mission becomes militarized as they fight their way through defenses to reach Fischer’s subconscious vault. Across all layers, time stretches and chaos builds. The van begins to fall off a bridge. The hotel loses gravity—Arthur fights in a rotating hallway as bodies float. Explosions ripple through the fortress as the team prepares synchronized kicks. Fischer is shot. He dies in the dream and falls into limbo instead of waking. Cobb makes a choice. He and Ariadne go after him. They descend into a vast, crumbling city—Cobb’s memory. Buildings decay into the ocean. This is where he and Mal once lived, lost in dreams for years. Cobb reveals the truth: Mal believed their world wasn’t real because Cobb planted that idea in her mind. COBB “I did it to bring us back… but it never left her.” Mal appears, calm but threatening. MAL “You’re still dreaming.” She tries to trap Cobb in guilt and keep him in limbo. Ariadne urges him to let go. Cobb confronts Mal directly. COBB “You’re not her. You’re just what I made.” He finally releases the projection. Mal collapses and fades. Fischer is found in a broken room. He approaches a safe—his subconscious. Inside, he finds a childhood memory of his dying father. FATHER “I’m disappointed… that you tried to be me.” Fischer breaks down. The idea takes hold—not rejection, but freedom. The kicks align. The fortress explodes. The hotel elevator drops. The van hits the water. Time collapses inward as they rise through the layers. Cobb remains behind briefly and finds Saito, now old again and lost in limbo. COBB “We had an agreement.” He reminds Saito of reality, of the mission, of the need to return. Back on the plane, everyone wakes. The tension dissolves quietly. Saito makes a phone call. Cobb passes through the airport. No one stops him. He arrives home to the same backyard and the same children. He spins the top. It spins. He walks away to embrace them. The top continues spinning, slightly wavering— but we never see it fall. CUT TO BLACK.
