Miles Morales continues balancing his life as a teenager in Brooklyn with his responsibilities as Spider-Man. Miles struggles to manage both identities as Spider-Man while dealing with pressure from his parents. Gwen Stacy, another Spider-Person, reconnects with Miles and brings him into a larger multiverse conflict. Gwen introduces Miles to many Spider-People who protect different universes. Miles meets Miguel and the Spider Society, where he learns about canon events that every Spider-Man must experience. One of these events involves Miles potentially losing his father. Miles refuses to accept this and decides to challenge the rules of the Spider Society. This leads to conflict between Miles and the other Spider-People, including Gwen. A villain called the Spot gains power and becomes a threat across multiple dimensions. As the Spot grows stronger, the situation becomes more dangerous for every Spider-Man. Miles escapes and travels across universes while being chased by the Spider Society. Gwen begins to question the system and decides to help Miles. Miles ends up in an alternate universe and faces a darker version of himself. Meanwhile, Gwen forms a team to rescue Miles as the story ends on a cliffhanger.
